# Team Charter — Northstar Sprint, Group 77

## Purpose
This charter sets out how Group 77 communicates, makes decisions, and handles blockers during the Northstar Sprint, so the team's process is transparent and auditable per the sprint's non-negotiable rules.

## Communication
- **Primary channel**: team group chat is used for day-to-day communication, blockers, and important updates.
- **Task/status tracking**: task-specific progress and status updates are kept on the GitHub Project board, not the group chat — the board is the single source of truth for task state.

## Response SLA
- Team members are expected to acknowledge important messages and questions within a reasonable timeframe during the working day.
- If unavailable, a member should communicate that in advance where possible.

## Board Update Rule
- Task statuses must be updated on the **same day** that work is completed or its status changes, so the board accurately reflects real progress at all times — matching the sprint's audit-log requirement.

## Escalation Rule
- If a team member is blocked, they should raise it as soon as possible rather than waiting.
- If an issue remains unresolved, or a team member shows **no visible activity for two days**, it is escalated according to this process:
  1. Direct check-in with the member via the group chat.
  2. If unresolved, flagged to the group as a whole for visibility.
  3. If still unresolved, raised per the program's standard escalation path.

## Conflict Resolution
- Disagreements on technical approach or task ownership are raised and discussed openly in the group chat first.
- If the team cannot reach agreement, the group lead makes the final call to keep the sprint on schedule, with reasoning shared back to the team.

## Commit / Edit Convention
- All commits follow: `<type>: <what changed> - <why it matters>`.
- Generic messages such as "wip" or "updates" are not acceptable.
